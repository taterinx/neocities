Whether it be a new game idea, art piece or prose, grab a journal and plan everything in extensive detail, making sure to research it thesis-style. Once you're done, find a new idea to violate. Rinse and repeat.

Tip #3: It Could be 20% Cooler

Approach everything with playful scrutiny, based on your own arbitrary ideas of what Cool is. If you don't like it, it's loser shit. If you like it, push it to the max. You don't need drama. If a social media carousel is trying to get a rise out of you, scroll away. If the comments section is rancid, scroll away. Emotionally invest in the things that really matter: friendship and steaze. And the yuck admin stuff.

Don't let any weird man with unresolved issues convince you that you are helpless or illogical or manic. Have faith in your individuality (hobbies, interests, dreams) and if they don't like it or are disappointed that it doesn't match their expectation of you then walk away.
