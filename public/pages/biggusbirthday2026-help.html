Biggus' Birthday Bash: The Tutorial

Welcome to my 6 Days of Biggus celebration. A lot of work was put into this site so I hope you have fun exploring it.

From the first day, there will be six unlockable photocards (see Vending Machine) already available, their codes hidden througout the birthday site. Each day, between November 6th and 12th, a new code will be hidden for you to find, with new content sometimes accompanying it. A special message awaits you should you successfully complete the final puzzle.

Tutorial: Vending Machine

  1. Poke around the webpage for codes that follow the format of [X-YYYYYY] or [YYYYYY], with a mix of letters and numbers.
  2. Enter the code into the vending machine, ommitting the square brackets and "X-" (e.g., [3-o9i4ii] becomes just o9i4ii), making sure it is in lowercase.
  3. Receive a Biggus photocard corresponding to the code or, if you play around with your findings, unlock a special message...
