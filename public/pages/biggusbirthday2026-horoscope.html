Messages from the stars

Interview by Bill Bagel

Biggus agreed to meet up at a dimly lit fondue restuarant and I'm almost certain it was to throw me off my tracks: champagne rain, oil splattered across the pages I can barely see, red light obfuscating my written function to begin with. It is clear they won't let me leave with any objective memory of the night.

Nevertheless, they understand that a conversation needs to be had and thus I am allowed to persist.

Q: does Bigwuh believe in Astrology?

"I'm going to word this weirdly so bare with me. I don't believe in fate but there is some level of trust I have in coincidences.

It's not lost on me that the advice is pretty general, but when advice or descriptions align with my experience, as broad as they are, I'm absolutely comforted by it.

It affirms that my intuition is sound, you know? Everyone likes when they're recognised for doing the right thing or being on the right path.

Sometimes there's just too many things to work on and it helps to have an external force suggest a focus."

Q: and what of heavenly bodies?

"I am one."

Q: How do you feel about your zodiac?

"I knew I was a Scorpio sun for the longest time, so that informed a lot of my self-perception growing up.

On its own, it was whatever but throughout my last relationship, I was reduced to those notorious traits that would be the scapegoats for our problems. Just an unfortunate coincidence really.

Leaving that, I had to learn to give some credit to those traits that he assigned as negative and in turn regain some pride."

Q: What negative traits?

"I'm just a beast... Nah but let's dissect the Scorpio mind, yeah: I'm intense when the people I care about do wrong by me. I'm a stubborn rat. I love intensely. I also LOVE cunninglingus."

Q: What of your other signs?

"Eventually I asked about my actual time of birth and found out I was a Sagittarius moon and Gemini rising.

Looking into it, Sagittarius moons thrive on some form of adventure, life can't get stagnant. Lately, I've felt quite in-tune with that idea. I'm comfortable where I'm at but I always need something shiny to walk towards.

Being a Gemini rising compliments those feelings quite well. I'm still a bit shy, I'll admit, but I'll take the complimentary description of being a lively, persuasive communicator, if not a freak."

Q: Do you like tha planets?

"Ahooo-hoo-hoo, I am totally indifferent. Space is crazy, though. I wasn't fixated but I'm very nostalgic for the trivia. At least until I think about it, then I get really freaked out by the physics business. False vacuums became a morbid research project one time.

Apparently my planet is Mars, God of Rawr."

I gulp. That concludes our interview. I wanted to say a lot more and it didn't help knowing I had the power to tip her location off to her persuers. Instead of indulging me any further, Biggus takes a final sip of their drink and excuses themselves to go to the bathroom. It has now been forty-five minutes... Staff are asking me to pay and leave.
